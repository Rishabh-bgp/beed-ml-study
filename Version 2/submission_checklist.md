## Still needs Rishabh (before upload)
1. **Double-blind vs guideline section 8.** The website says submissions get double-blind review, while the guidelines ask for full author details on page 1. Ask the organisers (conference@nielitbioai.org) which copy to upload; until then, upload the anonymised copy to CMT and keep the camera-ready copy for acceptance.
2. **Declarations:** finalise the Generative-AI declaration (keep it accurate: AI agents wrote the code, ran the experiments and drafted the text), and confirm the conflict-of-interest and funding statements.
3. **Authorship:** confirm the author list, author contributions, the corresponding author and ORCIDs; all authors must approve the final version (section 3 iv–v).
4. **Repository and licence:** create the public repository or archive DOI and choose a licence, then fill in [Repository URL / DOI], [DOI to be added] and [MIT or other licence].
5. **Originality:** confirm the work has not been published or submitted elsewhere (section 3 i–ii).
6. **Code zip:** section 4 allows only one DOCX and one PDF each for the manuscript and the supplement. Ask whether the code ZIP may be uploaded too; if not, rely on the repository link.
7. **Cover letter:** date, sign and remove the [Confirm] tags once the items above are settled.
8. **Registration:** at least one author must register after acceptance (author registration opens 15 Nov 2026, closes 20 Nov 2026).
9. Do **not** upload the presentation, speaker notes, explainer, README or CHANGES files; they are not submission files.
